FOOD DRIVE

"It's not how much we give but how much love we put into giving."

Welcome to the Community Food Drive! This webpage encourages everyone to participate in helping families who are facing hunger. Together, we can make a positive difference in our community.

HOW CAN YOU VOLUNTEER FOR THIS PROGRAM

Donate rice, flour, pulses, and canned food.

Volunteer to collect and distribute food.

Invite your friends and family for this good step.

REASONS TO VOLUNTEER FOR THIS PROGRAM

A volunteer joins a Food Drive to help reduce hunger and support families in need.

By volunteering, they contribute to collecting and distributing food to those who need it most.

It also allows them to develop teamwork, compassion, and a sense of social responsibility while making a positive impact on the community.
